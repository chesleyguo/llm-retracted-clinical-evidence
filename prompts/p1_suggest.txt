A clinical trial was retracted. Decide whether its claim is supported by reliable evidence other than the retracted trial, using the codebook definition and the search results below.

=== CODEBOOK DEFINITION (P1) ===
{p1_definition}

=== RETRACTED TRIAL ===
Title: {title}
Published: {published}
Retracted: {retracted}

=== CLAIM ===
{claim}

=== SEARCH RESULTS (up to 5 systematic reviews, meta-analyses or guidelines published after the trial) ===
{reviews}

How to judge:
- A review counts as independent support only if it supports the claim without depending on the retracted trial. A review that cites the retracted trial ("cites_retracted: YES") probably included it.
- The strongest independent evidence is a review published after the retraction that does not cite the trial.
- If cites_retracted is UNKNOWN, judge from the abstract (for example, the years or number of included trials) whether the review could have included the trial, and say so.
- YES only if at least one review that does not depend on the retracted trial supports this specific claim: it studies the same intervention and comparison, reports the same (or a very close) outcome, and finds an effect in the same direction. List only such reviews in basis_pmids.
- A review whose outcome or population clearly differs from the claim can support UNCLEAR at most, never YES or NO.
- An umbrella review ("umbrella_review: YES", a review of reviews or meta-analyses) may depend on the retracted trial without citing it, so it can support UNCLEAR at most.
- NO only if some review shows that the claim rests mainly on the retracted trial, or if independent evidence contradicts the claim; list those reviews in basis_pmids.
- UNCLEAR if the evidence is mixed, or if none of the reviews addresses this specific claim (same intervention, condition and outcome). Absence of evidence is UNCLEAR, not NO.

Return a JSON object:
{"suggested_p1": "YES|NO|UNCLEAR",
 "basis_pmids": ["PMIDs of the reviews the judgment rests on"],
 "rationale": "one sentence",
 "inclusion_notes": [{"pmid": "...", "likely_includes_retracted": "YES|NO|UNCLEAR", "note": "short reason"}]}
Give one inclusion note for every review listed.
